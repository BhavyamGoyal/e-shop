# Jeep Kit Card – Reference Spec

All sizes in mm, taken from the mesh in `Prints/Experiments/jeep_kit_card.3mf`. Part names are matched against the maker's photos.

## Overview
- **Source model:** "Jeep Kit Card" by agepbiz, MakerWorld model 223303. A WWII Willys-style Jeep supplied as a plastic-model sprue card; parts are snapped off and assembled into a toy.
- **Card size:** 97.4 × 55.0 mm, 10.7 mm tall overall.
- **Sprue frame:** rectangular border 1.6 mm thick, with parts hanging inside it on small runners.
- **Scale:** Jeep body is 48 mm long, about 1:70.
- **Print settings:** 0.16 mm layers, 2 walls, 15% infill, no supports, no raft, 0.4 mm nozzle (Bambu P1). Single-colour PLA (#638067 army green), 9.43 g, about 43 minutes.
- **Colour variants:** single-colour is all green. Multicolour has black tyres, tan seats and a white star on the bonnet.

## Layout (top view, long side horizontal)
- **Left end:** body tub and bonnet, with the star on the bonnet.
- **Middle, lower rows:** five wheels in two rows of three and two, each tyre around its hub.
- **Middle, upper rows:** chassis, with the windshield frame and bumper beside it.
- **Right of centre:** three seats stacked in a column, with the jerry can and steering wheel above them.
- **Right edge:** AGEPBIZ plate.

## Assembly
1. Cut every part off its runner and trim the stubs.
2. Press the chassis into the underside of the body.
3. Fit the axles into the four tyre/hub assemblies. The fifth goes on the back as the spare.
4. Drop the two front seats and the rear bench into the tub.
5. Fit the steering wheel and jerry can, then the windshield and bumper.

Exact peg and socket fits are not confirmed. The mesh only gives outer dimensions, so check clearances against the photos.

## Notes for the designer
- Keep a gap of about 0.2–0.3 mm between each part and its runner or socket. The original prints without supports.
- Keep wall thickness at 0.8 mm or more and runner thickness at 0.8 mm. Raised details are 0.4–0.6 mm and print cleanly.
- Details to match: star ring, grille, tyre tread, seat shapes, plate lettering. Replace "AGEPBIZ" with our own brand name.
